Lesson 14 — Lost!

Your plane has come down near a tiny island. There's no phone signal, the sun is hot and nobody knows where you are. What will you do? Today we talk about survival — and use the grammar that every survival guide is full of: if (If we drink seawater, we'll get even thirstier) and rules with must, have to, should. Part 1 · new grammar

If … , … will …

First conditional — a real possibility and its result: If we light a fire, a ship will see the smoke.

if + Present Simple, will + verb. Never will after if: ✗ If it will rain → ✓ If it rains, we'll build a shelter. The two halves can swap: We'll build a shelter if it rains. (no comma)

unless = if … not: We'll die of thirst unless we find water. · when = we're sure it will happen: When the sun goes down, it will get cold.

Exercise 3 · Grammar · new

Rules on the island

must / have to — necessary · mustn't — forbidden! · don't have to — not necessary (you can if you want) · should — good advice.

The big trapmustn't ≠ don't have to. You mustn't swim here = заборонено. You don't have to swim = не обов'язково.
  • You drink seawater — it can kill you.
  • We find fresh water in the next three days.
  • You sleep in the shade during the hottest hours — it's a good idea.
  • We walk far — there's a stream behind the trees.
  • You eat berries that you don't know.
  • Everyone stay in the group. That's the rule!
  • We build the shelter before it gets dark.
  • You wear shoes on the sand, but it's safer.

Exercise 5 · Reading

Eleven days in the jungle

A true story from 1971. Read it, then do the tasks.

On 24 December 1971, seventeen-year-old Juliane Koepcke got on a plane in Lima, the capital of Peru. She was flying with her mother to the Amazon rainforest, where her parents, two German scientists, had a research station. The day before, she had finished school. It was supposed to be a short Christmas flight.

During the flight, the plane flew into a terrible storm. It was hit by lightning and broke apart in the air. Juliane fell about three kilometres, still in her seat — and landed in the thick jungle alive. Experts think the seat and the trees slowed her fall. She had a broken collarbone, a deep cut on her arm and an eye injury, but she could walk. She was the only survivor of the crash.

Juliane didn't panic. She had spent a lot of time at the research station, and her parents had taught her the rules of the jungle. The most important one was simple: if you get lost, find water and follow it. If you follow a stream, it will lead you to a river — and if you find a river, you will find people.

So she walked through the water, day after day. She had almost no food, only a small bag of sweets. Insects bit her all the time, and the wound on her arm got infected. She knew she mustn't stop for long, and that walking in the water was easier than fighting through the thick forest.

After nine days she found a small hut on the river bank. She was too weak to go on, so she waited until a group of local forest workers came back to the hut. A few hours later, they cleaned her wound, took her down the river by boat, and she was flown to a hospital. She had survived.

Juliane later became a biologist, like her parents, and returned to the rainforest to work. She has always said that the jungle knowledge her parents gave her helped her to survive.

Граматика уроку

Умовні речення першого типу — про реальну можливість у майбутньому і її наслідок. Плюс модальні дієслова для правил і порад.

01 · first conditional

If + Present Simple, will + дієслово

If we light a fire, a ship will see the smoke.
If you don't wear a hat, you'll get sunstroke.
We'll build a shelter if it rains.

Українською кажемо «якщо піде дощ» (майбутній час), а англійською після if — теперішній: ✗ If it will rain → ✓ If it rains.

Кома: якщо речення починається з if — ставимо кому. Якщо if посередині — без коми.

Замість will можна might (можливо), can (зможеш) або наказ: If you see a snake, don't move.

02 · if · unless · when

Три слова — три значення

словозначенняприклад
ifякщо (може статися, а може й ні)If it rains, we'll stay inside.
unlessякщо неWe'll get lost unless we take a map. = if we don't take
whenколи (точно станеться)When the sun goes down, it'll get cold.

Після when теж теперішній час: ✗ when I will arrive → ✓ when I arrive.
