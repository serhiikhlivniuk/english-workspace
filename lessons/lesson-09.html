Warm-up B · Grammar review

Questions, questions

A journalist needs perfect questions. Make one from the words. Remember: question word + helper + person + verb. Watch the last one — who scored? needs no helper!

  • (you / play / football / every day)
  • (what / you / do / right now)
  • (where / you / be / last Saturday)
  • (what / you / do / when the goal was scored)
  • (you / ever / meet / a famous player)
  • (who / you / take after)
  • (what / you / look forward to)
  • (who / score / the winning goal)
Two rules for today1) The preposition stays at the end: Who do you take after? What are you looking forward to? 2) When who / what is the person who does the action, there's no do / did: Who scored? ✗ Who did score?

Exercise 1 · Reading

Maksym's press conference

Read the interview. New words are bold — most of them are clear from the sentence. Then do the tasks.

Words to help

come on — вийти на заміну · bench — лава запасних · tactics — тактика · equalise — зрівняти рахунок · injury time — компенсований час · opponent — суперник · on paper — формально, «на папері» · give credit to — віддати належне · celebrate — святкувати

Last Sunday Dnipro Juniors beat Lviv Academy 2–1 in the Under-17 Cup final. The winning goal came from 16-year-old Maksym Koval, who came on as a substitute in the second half. Here is part of his press conference.

Journalist: Maksym, congratulations! How do you feel right now?

Maksym: Honestly, I can't believe it yet. It's a dream come true. I've watched this final on TV every year since I was seven, and today I scored the winning goal. I don't think I'll sleep tonight!

Journalist: You started on the bench. Were you disappointed?

Maksym: A little bit, yes. But the coach explained his tactics before the match, and I agreed with him. Lviv were tired in the second half, so he needed fresh legs. I knew I had to be ready.

Journalist: Talk us through the goal.

Maksym: We were losing 1–0 until the 70th minute, when Denys equalised with a great header. After that we were much more confident. In injury time, Ivan passed the ball to me on the left. I was so tired that I could hardly feel my legs, but I didn't want to give up. I took one touch and shot. When I saw the ball in the net, I just ran to the fans.

Journalist: What was going through your mind?

Maksym: Nothing! My head was completely empty. Then I saw my dad in the stand — he was crying. That was the best moment of my life.

Journalist: People say you take after your dad. Is that true?

Maksym: (laughs) Maybe. He played for a local team when he was young, and he's the one who got me into football. He has never missed one of my matches, even in the rain. I can always rely on him.

Journalist: Lviv were the favourites. Is your team as good as theirs?

Maksym: On paper, no. They have more experienced players, and they've won this cup three times. But football doesn't depend only on talent. We worked harder than them today, and we believed in ourselves. I have to give credit to Lviv, though — they were a very tough opponent.

Journalist: Who do you look up to?

Maksym: Shevchenko, of course, like every Ukrainian kid. And Yamal — he's only a few years older than me, and he's already a superstar. That's crazy.

Journalist: And what's next for you?

Maksym: First, I'm going to celebrate with my teammates! Then I'm looking forward to a few days off. After that — back to training. One trophy is great, but I want more. We'll take it one game at a time.

True or false?

  • 1. Maksym played from the first minute.
  • 2. He was a bit disappointed to start on the bench.
  • 3. Dnipro scored the first goal of the match.
  • 4. Maksym scored with a header.
  • 5. Maksym's dad was at the match.
  • 6. Lviv have won the cup before.
  • 7. Maksym thinks his team had better players than Lviv.
  • 8. He's going on a long holiday after the final.

Exercise 4 · Speaking · role-play

Press conference

Pick a scenario. One of us is the player, the other is the journalist — then we swap and pick another one. Take one minute to prepare.

⚽ The hero: you scored the winner in the cup final😞 The bad day: you lost 4–0 and missed a penalty✈️ The big move: at 17 you've just signed for a big European club🩹 The comeback: your first match after a long injury
🎤 Journalist card
Ask at least six questions, all in different forms:
· Present Simple (Do you…?)
· Past Simple (When did you…?)
· Past Continuous (What were you thinking when…?)
· Present Perfect (Have you ever…?)
· as … as or a comparative
· two verbs from Lesson 8 (look up to, get over, rely on…)
⚽ Player card
Give full answers — never just yes or no.
· Use at least three phrases from Exercise 2.
· Add one detail to every answer: when, where, who, why.
· Use at least three verbs from Lesson 8.
· Stay in character — even when the question is difficult!

Bonus point for every question with a preposition at the end: Who did you dedicate the goal to? What are you worried about?

Граматика уроку

Урок-інтерв'ю — тож головне сьогодні питання: як їх правильно ставити в усіх часах, які ми знаємо. Плюс дві пастки: питання на підмет (Who scored?) і прийменник у кінці питання (Who do you look up to?).

Найчастіша помилка✗ Where you were born? → ✓ Where were you born? · ✗ When you started? → ✓ When did you start?
Допоміжне дієслово завжди стоїть перед «хто». Після did / does — дієслово в першій формі: ✗ did you started.
02 · пастка

Питання на підмет: Who scored?

Питаємо, ХТО зробив (who / what — підмет)
→ без do / did, порядок як у ствердженні:
Who scored the goal? — Maksym scored.
What happened in the second half?
Who got you into football?
Питаємо, КОГО / ЩО (who / what — додаток)
→ звичайне питання з do / did:
Who did Maksym thank? — He thanked his dad.
What did the coach say?

✗ Who did score the goal? → ✓ Who scored the goal?

03 · прийменник у кінці

Who do you look up to?

Коли в питанні є дієслово з прийменником (урок 8), прийменник залишається в кінці — він «прив'язаний» до дієслова:

Who do you look up to? · What are you looking forward to?
What does it depend on? · Who does this ball belong to?
What are you worried about? · Who do you get on with?

Українською прийменник іде на початок («На кого ти рівняєшся?»), але англійською в розмові так не кажуть. ✗ To who do you look up?
