Part 1 · new

Verbs that come with a partner

verb + small word = a new meaning. take = брати, but take after = бути схожим на. You can't guess it from the parts — learn the pair together, like one word.

1. The partner word is fixed: depend on, never depend from. 2. After a preposition the verb takes -ing: I'm looking forward to playing. 3. With some verbs a short pronoun goes in the middle: look it up, take them off.

Exercise 3 · Grammar · new

Preposition + -ing

After a preposition (on, in, for, about, at, to…) the verb takes -ing. Yes — even after look forward to! give up and carry on also take -ing.

The trap✗ I'm looking forward to see you. → ✓ I'm looking forward to seeing you. In look forward to, to is a preposition, not part of the infinitive.
  • I'm looking forward to in the final. (play)
  • Our team succeeded in in the last minute. (score)
  • He apologised for late. (be)
  • She's worried about the bus. (miss)
  • Have you ever thought about up tennis? (take)
  • I gave up chips before matches. (eat)
  • Carry on — don't stop! (run)
  • He's good at penalties. (take)
  • Thanks for me with my English! (help)

Talent isn't everything

Exercise 6 · Reading

What makes a good player?

A youth coach writes about what really matters. Today's verbs are bold. Read, then do the tasks.

I have been a youth football coach for almost twenty years, and parents always ask me the same question: "Is my son talented enough?" My honest answer is: it depends on what you mean by talent.

Every September about thirty new kids turn up for our first training session. Some of them took up football the summer before; others have played since they could walk. In the first few weeks the difference between them is huge. But talent is not nearly as important as most people think. Over the years I have found out that three other things matter more.

The first is attitude. The players who make it are not always the fastest or the strongest. They are the ones who carry on when things go wrong. I remember a boy called Taras. He was smaller than everyone else, and in his first season he hardly ever got the ball. Lots of kids give up in that situation. Taras didn't. He stayed after training, practised his passing and looked up every exercise he didn't understand. Five years later he took over as captain.

The second is the team. You can't rely on one star. A team where the players get on with each other and look after each other is almost always stronger than eleven stars who only care about themselves. I have seen it a hundred times.

The third is the ability to get over a bad day. Every player makes mistakes, and every team loses. The best young players don't complain about the referee or the pitch — they focus on what they can do better next time.

People often ask me who my players look up to. Twenty years ago almost everyone wanted to be Shevchenko. Today it's Mbappé, Yamal or Haaland. I always tell them: don't just copy your heroes' tricks — copy their discipline.

And the parents? Kids usually take after them more than they think. A calm parent on the touchline means a calm player on the pitch. So my advice is simple: believe in your child, don't worry about the score, and enjoy the game. The trophies can wait.

— Oleh, youth coach, Kyiv

Граматика уроку

Дієслова, які завжди ходять «у парі» з маленьким словом. Головне правило: вчимо пару цілком, як одне слово, — і з прикладом.

01 · що це

Дієслово + частка = нове значення

Фразове дієслово — дієслово + частка (up, off, over, after…). Значення часто зовсім нове.
take — брати → take after — бути схожим на
give — давати → give up — кинути, здатися
Дієслово + прийменник — значення зрозуміле, але прийменник фіксований, і часто не такий, як в українській.
залежати від → depend on
погоджуватися з → agree with

04 · після прийменника

Прийменник + -ing

preposition + verb-ing
I'm good at taking penalties. · He apologised for being late. · Thanks for helping.

Найпідступніше — look forward to: тут to — прийменник, тому ✗ looking forward to see you → ✓ looking forward to seeing you.

Так само з -ing: give up (кинути щось робити) і carry on (продовжувати): He gave up eating sweets. Carry on running!

05 · де стоїть займенник

look it up, але look after him

Можна розірвати (take up, take off, look up, find out, give up):
Take off your boots. = Take your boots off.
Займенник — тільки посередині: Take them off. ✗ Take off them.
Не розриваємо (take after, look after, look for, get over, усі «дієслово + прийменник»):
I look after him. ✗ I look him after.
She takes after her mum.

Не впевнений — став об'єкт після всієї пари. Для A2+/B1 цього досить.
