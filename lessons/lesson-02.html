Adverbs of frequency

Where to put always, usually, often and never — and why that position is not random.

The main rule

before the main verb, but AFTER the verb to be

always · usually · often · sometimes · hardly ever · never

I always do my homework.

He is never late.

With an auxiliary verb

auxiliary + adverb + main verb

When there is can, will, have, or am/is/are + -ing.

She can usually help me.

Frequency expressions

once a week · twice a month · every day — at the start or end of the sentence

These expressions never go in the middle of the sentence.

We train twice a week.

Watch out

I go always to school by bus.I always go to school by bus.
He never is tired.He is never tired.

Exercise 7 · GrammarPresent Simple vs Continuous

Routine or right now?

Put the verb in brackets into the Present Simple or the Present Continuous. Short forms (I'm, isn't, don't) are fine.

Present Simple vs Present Continuous

The core question is simple: is this generally true, or is it happening around now?

The one question to ask

generally true → Present Simple · happening around now → Present Continuous

Ask yourself: would this still be true next month? If yes, it is a routine and takes the Simple.

I walk to school. (every day, in general)

I'm walking to school. (right now, on my way)

Time markers give it away

Simple: always · usually · every day · on Mondays · never Continuous: now · right now · at the moment · today · this week

Find the time expression first — it usually decides the tense for you.

She usually wears jeans, but today she's wearing a dress.

Both tenses in one sentence

…usually + Present Simple, but + today / at the moment + Present Continuous

This is the pattern that shows you really understand the contrast — use it in writing and speaking.

I normally have breakfast at home, but this week I'm eating at school.

My brother plays football, but he isn't playing today — he's ill.

Careful: state verbs

know · like · want · need · understand · believe — Simple only

Even when you mean right now, state verbs stay in the Simple. See the State verbs topic.

I want to go home now. (NOT I'm wanting)

Watch out

Look! He plays the guitar.Look! He's playing the guitar.
I'm going to school every day.I go to school every day.
She is knowing the answer.She knows the answer.
What do you do right now?What are you doing right now?

Careful with number 8

Verbs like like, love, hate, know, want, need, understand, prefer describe a state, not an action. We do not normally use them in the Continuous: He is liking rock music. → He likes rock music.

Exercise 9 · Reading

“I fell off after two seconds”

I can't remember exactly when I became obsessed with skateboarding. I think I was about ten. My cousin was riding an old board outside our house one afternoon, and I asked him if I could try it. I fell off after two seconds. Everybody laughed — and I was hooked.

These days I go to the skate park four or five times a week. It isn't really a hobby any more; it's a routine. At the moment I'm learning a trick called a kickflip and, honestly, it's driving me crazy. I started in March and I still land on the ground more often than on the board.

Last summer I had my worst accident. I was going down a ramp quite fast when my wheel hit a stone. I broke my wrist and spent six weeks in a cast. My mum wanted me to give up. I didn't.

I also play the drums, but only at weekends, because our neighbours can't stand the noise. People often ask me why I never play video games. The truth is, I find them a bit pointless. Sitting inside while the sun is shining feels like a waste of a day — although, to be fair, this is Manchester, so the sun isn't shining very often.

— Danny, 14, Manchester

Довідник для учня і для вчителя. Тримайте цю вкладку відкритою в окремому вікні під час уроку: до неї зручно повертатися, коли в мовленні виникає помилка.

Коли: рутина, звичка, розклад, факт, який правдивий взагалі. Відповідає на питання How often?

+   I / you / we / they play · he / she / it plays
−   I don't play · he doesn't play
?   Do you play? · Does he play?
usuallyalwaysneverevery daytwice a weekat weekendsafter school

Позиція прислівника: перед смисловим дієсловом, але після to be.

I usually play football.
✓ перед дієсловом
I am always tired.
✓ після am / is / are

Вирази частотності (twice a week, every day) стоять у кінці речення.

✗ I play usually football.   ✗ He don't play.   ✗ Does he plays?
