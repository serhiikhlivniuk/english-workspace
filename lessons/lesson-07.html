Part 1 · new

as … as: the same, nearly, not nearly

as + adjective + as = the same. not as … as = less. Add one word in front to show how big the difference is.

just as fast as (exactly the same) · nearly / almost as fast as (a tiny bit less) · not quite as fast as (a bit less) · not nearly as fast as (much less) · twice / three times as fast as

Messi is not nearly as tall as Ronaldo. Pelé won three times as many World Cups as Messi. He doesn't play as well as his brother.

Exercise 1 · GrammarFrom Lesson 6

as … as

Same meaning, different words. Finish the second sentence.

as … as · not as … as

as + adjective + as = the same. not as … as = less.

The same

as + adjective + as

Two things are equal.

My brother is as tall as my dad now.

Less

not as + adjective + as

The first thing is less. Often a polite way to compare.

The bus isn't as fast as the train.

Same meaning, two ways

The bus is slower than the train. = The bus isn't as fast as the train.

Good for exam transformations.

Pietro's isn't as tasty as La Bella Napoli.

Watch out

as tall thanas tall as
not as taller asnot as tall as

Exercise 2 · Grammar · new

How big is the difference?

Look at the numbers and choose the right word.

just as · nearly as · not nearly as · twice as

One small word in front of as … as tells you how big the difference is.

Exactly the same

just as + adjective + as

just = exactly. Very common in speaking.

The new striker is just as fast as the old one.

A tiny bit less

nearly / almost as … as not quite as … as

The difference is small.

Our goalkeeper is nearly as tall as theirs: 1.88 and 1.90.

Much less

not nearly as … as

The difference is big.

A school match isn't nearly as exciting as a final.

Two, three times more

twice / three times as … as twice as many + noun + as

many with things you can count (goals, fans), much with things you can't (money, time).

Their stadium is twice as big as ours.

Pelé won three times as many World Cups as Messi.

Exercise 6 · Reading

Who is the greatest of all time?

Read Danylo's blog post. Don't stop at every new word — the meaning is usually clear from the sentence. Then do the tasks below.

Every football fan has an opinion about the GOAT — the Greatest Of All Time. In my class the argument never stops, so I decided to look at the facts instead of just shouting.

For most people, the debate comes down to two players: Lionel Messi and Cristiano Ronaldo. For more than fifteen years they dominated world football, and each of them pushed the other to become better. Their styles, however, are completely different. Messi is not nearly as tall as Ronaldo — he is 1.70 m, whereas Ronaldo is 1.87 m — but his dribbling is unbelievable: when he has the ball, defenders look as if they are standing still. Ronaldo, on the other hand, is an athlete who has built his career on hard work and discipline. He is just as dangerous with his head as with either foot, and he has scored more international goals than any other man in history.

What about trophies? Messi has won the Ballon d'Or eight times, which is more than any other player. Ronaldo has won it five times — not as many as Messi, but still more than anyone else in history. In 2022, Messi finally lifted the World Cup with Argentina, the one trophy that was missing from his collection. Ronaldo has never won it, although he won the European Championship with Portugal in 2016.

But is it fair to compare only modern players? My grandad always says that nobody will ever be as good as Pelé, who won three World Cups with Brazil — three times as many as Messi. My dad, by contrast, believes that Maradona was the most talented player who has ever lived, despite his difficult character.

We have our own legend, too. In 2004 Andriy Shevchenko became the first player from independent Ukraine to win the Ballon d'Or. He was never as famous as Messi or Ronaldo, but for thousands of Ukrainian kids he was the reason they started playing.

And then there is the next generation. Lamine Yamal became the youngest player ever to score at a European Championship when he was only sixteen. Is he as good as Messi was at his age? It's too early to say.

So who is the greatest? For me, it's Messi, because no other player has ever made football look as easy as he has. But ask me again in ten years — my answer might be different.

— Danylo, 15, Off the Pitch blog

Notice: whereas · although · despite

Three parks, one winner?

Read Max's review and answer: which park?

Our town has three skate parks, and my friends and I have skated at all of them, so here's my honest review.

Riverside Park is the oldest. It opened in 2009 and it looks really old-fashioned now — the ramps are cracked, and in winter it's the coldest place in town because of the wind from the river. But it's free, and it's never crowded, so it's the most peaceful of the three.

Central Skate Zone is the newest and the most modern. The ramps are huge, and the bowl is much bigger than the one at Riverside. The problem is the price: an hour costs 80 hryvnias, so it's by far the most expensive. And on Saturdays it's so crowded that you wait ten minutes for every run.

The School Park is right behind my school, so for me it's the most convenient. It's smaller than Central, but it's safer than Riverside because the surface is new. It costs 30 hryvnias — cheaper than Central, but obviously not as cheap as Riverside.

So which one is the best? For learning new tricks, Central, no question. For hanging out with friends after lessons, the School Park. And when I just want to skate alone and think? Riverside. I've skated at all three hundreds of times, and I still can't choose just one.

Граматика уроку

Головна тема — as … as: як сказати «такий самий», «майже такий», «зовсім не такий» і «вдвічі більший». Плюс три слова-зв'язки з тексту: whereas, although, despite.

01 · основа

as … as і not as … as

as + прикметник + as — такий самий, як
My brother is as tall as my dad.
not as (so) + прикметник + as — не такий, як (менш)
The bus isn't as fast as the train.

У середині — звичайна форма прикметника, без -er і без more: ✗ as taller as ✗ not as more expensive as → ✓ as tall as ✓ not as expensive as.

Ніколи ✗ as … than: than — тільки після вищого ступеня (taller than).

Одна думка — два способиRonaldo is taller than Messi. = Messi isn't as tall as Ronaldo.
Увага: міняємо місцями предмети і беремо протилежний прикметник або звичайний: slower than = not as fast as. Це улюблене завдання на екзаменах.
02 · нове

Наскільки велика різниця?

слово перед asзначенняприклад
just as … asточно такий самийThe new striker is just as fast as the old one.
nearly / almost as … asмайже такий (трохи менше)Our goalkeeper is nearly as tall as theirs.
not quite as … asне зовсім такий (трохи менше)Ours isn't quite as tall as theirs.
not nearly as … asзовсім не такий (набагато менше)Messi is not nearly as tall as Ronaldo.
twice / three times as … asудвічі / утричі більшеTheir stadium is twice as big as ours.
Пастка✗ two times bigger as → ✓ twice as big as. «Удвічі» — це twice, а не two times (у розмові можна two times bigger than, але twice as … as — правильніше і звучить краще).
03 · нове

as many / as much / the same … as

as many + те, що можна порахувати
We scored as many goals as them.
Pelé won three times as many World Cups as Messi.
as much + те, що не рахується
I don't have as much time as you.
He earns twice as much money as his teammates.

the same + іменник + as — такий самий: I'm the same age as him. You've got the same boots as me. ✗ the same like → ✓ the same as.

Після as у розмові кажуть me / him / her: He's as tall as me. Формальніше — as tall as I am.

04 · нове

Прикметник чи прислівник?

be, look, seem + прикметник
He is as good as his brother.
The match wasn't as exciting as I expected.
дія (play, run, pass) + прислівник
He plays as well as his brother.
Pass the ball as quickly as you can.
quick → quickly · calm → calmly · bad → badly · careful → carefully
good → well · fast → fast · hard → hard (без -ly!)

✗ He plays as good as me. → ✓ He plays as well as me.
